Work

  1. Spurious factor detection under nonstationary covariance

    Jul 2026
    Pilot study

    A standard way to clean a correlation matrix works out the range of values pure noise would produce, treats everything inside that range as random, and keeps anything above it as a real factor. In simulation I found that when volatility comes in bursts and correlations shift once, data with no real factor in it can produce a value about 20 times the top of that range, so the cleaning keeps a factor that isn't there.

    • The largest eigenvalue reaches 44.2, against a noise edge of 2.25.
    • A closed-form formula for the spike is accurate to 1.4% in simulation.
    • On 471 S&P 500 constituents through the August 2011 US downgrade, the formula is within 12%.
    • Portfolios built on cleaning that is fooled this way carry 50 to 65% more out-of-sample risk.
    • Weighting recent data more heavily (EWMA) does not fix it.

    The ideas in this paper are mine; the write-up had AI help.

    Code and paper for Spurious factor detection under nonstationary covariance

  2. Systematic trading system

    2026
    Active

    A trading system running on an Alpaca paper account. The repository bundles daily SPX, bitcoin and crude oil (WTI) history and the backtest script, so anyone can rerun it.
